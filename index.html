Privacy Policy of the Hedron App

The Hedron app does not collect personal data.

The Hedron app is sandboxed and has very limited access to your Mac.

  • Printing
  • Read and write access only to files you select
  • The help inside the app is displayed from a local file. Outgoing network access is only used to show the Wikipedia and Apple pages linked from this help. All other links are opened in your default web browser.

  • No incoming network access
  • No camera access
  • No microphone access
  • No USB access
  • No Bluetooth access
  • No access to your contacts
  • No access to your location
  • No access to your calendar
